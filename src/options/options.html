Adaptive Playback

Videos are never sped up on their own. Each one is read and planned in the background, and stays at normal speed until you press Enable in the toolbar popup.

Pace

Relaxed Brisk

Faster passages are slowed and slower ones are sped up so speech reaches you at a steady pace. The estimate above comes from running a real 40 minute lecture through the same engine the extension uses.

Model provider

Any endpoint that speaks the OpenAI chat completions API works here, including OpenRouter, Groq, and a local Ollama or LM Studio server.

Your key is stored locally and sent only to the endpoint you configure. Transcripts are sent to that endpoint for analysis. Local storage protects against casual inspection, not against a compromised browser profile.

Analyzed videos

Difficulty ratings are stored on this computer and reused whenever you rewatch a video, so a video is never paid for twice. Clearing them means the next watch pays again.
